Stream 01 • On-Device Small LLM Next-Word Prediction

Perplexity & Predicted-Word Comparison

We run a lightweight language model directly on your device. Reading forward from the opening half of the passage, it predicts a ranked list of the exact next words it would write next. We then compare the author's actual next words against those predictions — AI writing routinely falls near the top of the model's expected choices with low surprise.
